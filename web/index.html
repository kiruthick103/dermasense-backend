How DermaSense Works

Deterministic priority ladder, photo quality standards, and pattern strength ratings explained.

Deterministic Priority Hierarchy

DermaSense executes in a strict order to ensure emergency signs always take precedence:

  1. Priority 1 (Emergency Danger Signs): Any warning sign immediately routes to Category D. Image patterns are hidden to prevent delay.
  2. Priority 2 (Contradictory Answers): Conflicting answers default to Category C for clinical recheck.
  3. Priority 3 (Steroid Risk Score ≥ 3): Unprescribed topical steroid use routes to Category B for treatment review.
  4. Priority 4 (Poor Image Quality / Non-Skin): Inadequate lighting or focus routes to Category C.
  5. Priority 5 (Fungal Pattern Score ≥ 2): Fungal-type ring pattern meeting strength criteria routes to Category A.
  6. Priority 6 (Otherwise): Routes to Category C for routine check.

Data Privacy & Storage Commitments

Information lifecycle, security safeguards, and complete data deletion protocols.

1. Zero Unsolicited Data Retention

By default, your rash photographs, questionnaire responses, and screening outcomes are processed entirely in temporary device memory. Nothing is written to permanent storage unless you explicitly check the consent boxes on Screen 1.

2. Offline Architecture

DermaSense includes a client-side deterministic vision and scoring engine (`vision.js`). Even if your device is completely disconnected from the Internet, the triage engine executes locally in your browser.

Accessibility Statement

Conformance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA and Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0).

DermaSense has been built to be universally accessible to all citizens, including persons with visual, auditory, motor, or cognitive disabilities:

  • Keyboard Navigation: All controls, forms, and camera interactions are fully operable via standard keyboard tabs and shortcuts.
  • Text Resizer: Controls to scale text size (A-, A, A+) are provided in the utility header.
  • High Contrast Mode: A dedicated toggle increases color contrast beyond 7:1 for enhanced readability.
  • Semantic HTML & ARIA: Landmarks, descriptive form fieldsets, legends, and `aria-live` regions communicate status changes to screen readers.
  • No Color As Sole Signal: Every triage category pairs color badges with explicit letters, textual urgency levels, and icons.
