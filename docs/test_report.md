## 1. Executive Summary

DermaSense has been upgraded into a government-hospital-style public health portal conforming to **WCAG 2.1 AA** and the **Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0)**. The portal operates offline via client-side pure engines and provides server-enforced role-based access control for community screening and clinical triage referral.

### Clinical & Regulatory Boundaries
- **NEVER diagnoses, prescribes, or advises medication changes.**
- **ZERO reassuring statements** (*"you are fine"*, *"nothing to worry about"* are banned by string scanner).
- Prominent disclaimers on header ribbon and footer: *"Prototype screening aid. Not an official government or hospital service."*
- Condition names appear strictly inside the *"Looks similar to (not a diagnosis)"* panel.
